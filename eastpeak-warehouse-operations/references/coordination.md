# Logistics and factory messages

Match the requested channel/action: chat text, native Gmail draft, or Alibaba message. Alibaba drafts should be concise without email ceremony unless requested. Drafting does not authorize sending.

When editing a compose window, read current recipients/subject/body immediately before applying the edit and preserve the user's changes. Use observed recipients; do not add autocomplete suggestions unasked. Verify content and leave unsent when requested.

## Warehouse + forwarder email

Include order reference, exact scope/units, origin and destination addresses. Ask the warehouse to reply all to the forwarder with final packing list, carton/pallet counts, gross weights and dimensions of each unit and total weight, ready date, exact pickup address, warehouse contact, pickup hours, appointment/booking reference and loading requirements.

Ask the forwarder to coordinate directly, confirm quote/pickup/transit/ETA and identify needed customs documents. Treat arrival deadlines as targets until confirmed. Do not imply stock is packed or released if it is still a draft.

When requested, label HS codes as recorded in product/source records and ask the broker to confirm destination extensions. Distinguish inventory-transfer item prices from customs values.

## Factory / Alibaba country split

Provide exact per-SKU quantities and totals by country, packing assumptions, addresses, receiving references and the correct label file for each destination. Preserve leading-zero identifiers such as 0026.

Explain label placement and blank fields. Separate countries; label mixed-carton inner bags and show actual contents on outer packing lists. Distinguish SKU labels from destination/carrier labels.

Request confirmation of quantities, dispatch dates, separate packing lists, final carton counts/weights/dimensions and label photos as appropriate to the task. Reference the allocation sheet and supplied labels, but never claim attachments are uploaded without verifying them.
